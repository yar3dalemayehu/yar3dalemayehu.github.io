RunoutMatches people by music taste, using their Spotify listening.

Sign in with Spotify and Runout compares your top artists and genres with other listeners. The score is 60% artist overlap (Jaccard similarity) plus 40% genre similarity (cosine, weighted by how much you listen to each). Spotify's development mode caps the app at 5 users, so it's a small-circle app for now. Letterboxd is the planned second half.
